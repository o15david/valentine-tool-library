How it works

Borrowing and lending in three steps, with a few ground rules.

1

Find and request

Search the catalog and send a borrow request with your name, contact, and the dates you need the tool.

2

Owner approves

The owner approves the request and arranges pickup with you. Most loans run 3 days, and owners set their own terms.

3

Return it ready

Bring it back clean, fueled or charged, and on time, so the next neighbor can use it. Either the owner or the borrower can mark it returned here.

Library ground rules

  • Neighbors lend to neighbors: borrowers and lenders both live in Valentine.
  • No fees. If something breaks in normal use, tell the owner right away and work it out in good faith.
  • Return tools clean and ready for the next person, by the due date on your loan.
  • Chainsaws and other high risk tools are lent at the owner's discretion, sometimes only with a quick orientation.
  • The Valentine Neighborhood Association hosts the catalog. Loans are private agreements between the two neighbors involved.
